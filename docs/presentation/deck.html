01 — The problem

Businesses adopted AI.
They didn't get an AI operation.

Small and mid-size ventures use chatbots as clever text boxes. The work around them — context, follow-through, approvals, memory — is still manual.

∅

No memory

Every chat starts from zero. Voice, clients, decisions and past work must be re-explained.

1:1

One-shot answers

Chatbots reply; they don't run multi-step workflows, hand off between specialists or chase outcomes.

$↑

Wrong model, wrong cost

A flagship model formats a date; a cheap model drafts strategy. No one routes by task.

🔒

Lock-in & risk

Knowledge lives inside a vendor. No audit trail, no approval gates, no ownership.

The gap: owners need an operations layer — agents that share one knowledge base, act under governance, and belong to the business, not the model vendor.

02 — Why it's hard

An "AI employee" is six hard problems stacked on top of each other

6  Governance — who may do what, with approval & audit
5  Security — prompt injection, secrets, RBAC
4  Orchestration — missions, handoffs, retries
3  Model economics — 4 providers, cost/quality
2  Memory — what to recall, when, how cheaply
1  Business knowledge — structured, owned, portable

Each layer depends on the one below it. Get knowledge wrong and memory is noise; get memory wrong and orchestration drifts; skip governance and nobody can trust the output.

06 — AI solutions

Right model, right agent, right moment

Multi-LLM routing

Every task is classified, then sent to the cheapest model that can do it well.

Simple · lookups, formattingGemini Flash
Content · writing, analysisClaude Sonnet
Complex · strategy, reasoningClaude Opus
Private · sensitive dataOllama (local)

Bar = relative capability / cost tier (illustrative)

Composable agents

Scope, inputs, outputs, guardrails and allow-listed tools. Hot-reloaded from disk. Each carries a persistent SOUL: role, personality, expertise.

Pipelines & 7 handoff types

Standard, QA-pass / QA-fail, escalation, phase-gate, sprint, incident — with Dev→QA retry loops.

Dreaming

Background reflex & curator cycles propose knowledge updates into a Dream Inbox — a human approves before anything becomes "truth".
