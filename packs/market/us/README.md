# United States market pack

English terms, defaults (USD, imperial, Monday to Friday, en-US) and ten rule cards on AIA pay applications, federal prompt payment and Miller Act bonds, mechanics liens, retainage, energy codes, contractor licensing and local permits.

**Verification: level 1 (drafted by an agent).** Federal cards cite acquisition.gov and govinfo.gov; state-law cards cite industry overviews and name one state as an example. No US professional has reviewed this pack.

**Missing:** state-by-state packs (state law decides liens, retainage, prompt payment and licensing), sales tax on materials, AIA A201 general conditions, and OSHA duties.

**Who could review it:** a construction attorney in the project state, and a licensed general contractor for practice.
