statement: Under the Construction Industry Scheme, contractors deduct 20% from the labour part of payments
  to registered subcontractors, 30% for unregistered ones and 0% for those with gross payment status,
  and pay it to HMRC.

Materials and VAT are excluded from the deduction. Verify each subcontractor's status with HMRC before the first payment.
