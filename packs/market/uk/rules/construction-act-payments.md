id: uk.construction-act.payment-notices
statement: Under the Housing Grants, Construction and Regeneration Act 1996 (as amended in 2011), construction
  contracts must provide stage payments, a payment notice for each due sum, and a pay less notice with
  its basis if the payer intends to pay less; without a pay less notice the notified sum must be paid.

If the payer issues no payment notice, the payee can issue a default notice. Contracts that don't comply fall back on the Scheme for Construction Contracts. Track every due date, notice and final date for payment.
