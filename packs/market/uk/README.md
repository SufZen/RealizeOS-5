# United Kingdom market pack

English terms, defaults (GBP, metric, Monday to Friday, en-GB) and ten rule cards on VAT, Construction Act payment notices and adjudication, the Construction Industry Scheme, Defective Premises Act limitation, higher-risk buildings, EPCs, SDLT, JCT and NEC, and the two approval regimes. England unless a card says otherwise.

**Verification: level 1 (drafted by an agent).** Sources are gov.uk, legislation.gov.uk and law firms; each card says which. No UK professional has reviewed this pack.

**Missing:** Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland differences, retention practice, CDM 2015 duties, Part L and other approved documents, and the Building Safety Levy.

**Who could review it:** a construction solicitor for the Construction Act and contracts, a chartered quantity surveyor for payments and CIS, and an approved inspector for approvals.
