# Cyprus market pack

Greek and English terms, defaults (EUR, Monday to Friday, el-CY) and ten rule cards on VAT (including the 2026 changes), stamp duty, transfer fees, depositing the contract of sale, title-deed delays, permits and final approval, contractor and engineer registration, and energy certificates.

**Verification: level 1 (drafted by an agent).** Several sources are secondary (law firms, tax news, developers); each card says where. No Cypriot professional has reviewed this pack.

**Missing:** construction contract practice, defect liability, capital gains and immovable property tax, the north/south title issues, and public procurement (Law 73(I)/2016).

**Who could review it:** a Cypriot advocate for sales, deposits and title deeds, a tax adviser for VAT and fees, and a registered civil engineer for permits.
