statement: A buyer should deposit the contract of sale at the Land Registry within six months of signing
  (Sale of Immovable Property (Specific Performance) Law 81(I)/2011); the deposit creates an encumbrance
  protecting the buyer, and later deposits need a court order.

Since the 2023 amendment (132(I)/2023), on mortgaged property the court grants specific performance only once the full price is paid. Track the deposit deadline as a milestone.
