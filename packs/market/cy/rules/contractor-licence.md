id: cy.contractors.registration
statement: Building and civil engineering contractors in Cyprus must be on the Contractors' Register and
  hold an annual licence from the Council for the Registration and Control of Building and Civil Engineering
  Contractors (Law 29(I)/2001); projects are classified by type and value, and contractors may only take
  work within their class.
