# Review sheet: Cyprus market pack (cy@0.1.0)

Verification level 1 (drafted). 10 rule cards, 2 glossary lists and 1 defaults row. Fill in `review-sheet.csv`, or write on this printout and send it back.

## How to review (about 60 minutes)

An AI agent drafted this pack from public sources. It is labelled verification level 1 (a draft). Your review lets it be marked as reviewed (level 2), with your name on each card you review.

For each card, check:

1. Is the statement correct and current for Cyprus?
2. Does the source support it? A better or official source is welcome.
3. Is anything important missing, misleading or out of date?

Then fill in:

- verdict: correct, corrected or removed.
- facts corrected: how many separate facts you changed (0 if correct). facts checked is our estimate of the facts in the statement; change it if you count differently.
- corrected statement: your wording, if you corrected the card.
- reviewer note: anything else, such as a better source or an exception.

The glossary and defaults rows are optional. Your name, title and registration number are recorded on every card you review.

## Πώς γίνεται ο έλεγχος (περίπου 60 λεπτά)

*Μετάφραση από πράκτορα τεχνητής νοημοσύνης, με βάση τις αγγλικές οδηγίες.*

Σε κάθε κάρτα ελέγξτε:

1. Είναι η διατύπωση σωστή και επίκαιρη για αυτή την αγορά;
2. Την τεκμηριώνει η πηγή; Μια καλύτερη ή επίσημη πηγή είναι ευπρόσδεκτη.
3. Λείπει κάτι σημαντικό, ή υπάρχει κάτι παραπλανητικό ή ξεπερασμένο;

Στη συνέχεια συμπληρώστε (γράψτε τις λέξεις της κρίσης στα αγγλικά):

Οι γραμμές του γλωσσαρίου και των προεπιλογών είναι προαιρετικές. Το όνομά σας, ο τίτλος σας και ο αριθμός μητρώου σας καταγράφονται σε κάθε κάρτα που ελέγχετε.

### 1. cy.contractors.registration

**Statement:** Building and civil engineering contractors in Cyprus must be on the Contractors' Register and hold an annual licence from the Council for the Registration and Control of Building and Civil Engineering Contractors (Law 29(I)/2001); projects are classified by type and value, and contractors may only take work within their class.

### 5. cy.sales.deposit-contract

**Statement:** A buyer should deposit the contract of sale at the Land Registry within six months of signing (Sale of Immovable Property (Specific Performance) Law 81(I)/2011); the deposit creates an encumbrance protecting the buyer, and later deposits need a court order.

> Since the 2023 amendment (132(I)/2023), on mortgaged property the court grants specific performance only once the full price is paid. Track the deposit deadline as a milestone.

- Source: <https://www.mondaq.com/cyprus/real-estate/788808/new-legislation-article-under-real-estate>

### 10. cy.vat.rates

**Statement:** Cyprus's standard VAT rate on new residential property is 19%; a reduced 5% rate can apply to an individual's primary residence on the first 130 m², up to €350,000, with a total area cap of 190 m², a €475,000 transaction cap, a ten-year occupancy condition and an application to the Tax Department.

> Amendments effective 1 September 2026 changed the definition of a new building and the conditions; a Tax Department notice of May 2026 allowed 200 m² for some earlier planning applications. Confirm each case with the Tax Department.

- Source: <https://gk-lawfirm.com/publications/new-reduced-scheme-5-vat-on-property-cyprus/>
- As of: 2026-10-13; review by: 2027-04-13
