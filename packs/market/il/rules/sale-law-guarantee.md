id: il.sale-law.buyer-funds-secured
statement: Under the Sale (Apartments) (Securing Investments of Apartment Buyers) Law, 1974, a developer
  may not take more than 7% of the price from a buyer of a new apartment unless the money is secured,
  most often by a bank guarantee.
applies_to:
  family: requirement
  kinds:
  - covenant

The threshold was lowered from 15% to 7% by Amendment 4 (2008). Other permitted securities exist (for example insurance or a registered caveat); the guarantee is the common one.
Agents flag any buyer payment schedule that collects more than 7% before a security is recorded. A lawyer confirms.
