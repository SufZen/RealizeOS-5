# Portugal market pack

Portuguese and English terms, defaults (EUR, metric, Monday to Friday, pt-PT) and ten rule cards for developers, contractors and buyers in mainland Portugal: IVA, price revision, complementary works, contractor licences, energy certificates, stamp duty and IMT, and Simplex Urbanístico 2.0.

**Verification: level 1 (drafted by an agent).** This pack was drafted by an agent from public sources, following `docs/drafting-a-market-pack.md`. Every card links to its source and has an as-of date. Some sources are secondary (press or law-firm summaries); those cards say so. No Portuguese professional has reviewed it yet.

**To raise it to level 2:** a named Portuguese advogado, solicitador or engenheiro reviews each card, records corrections, and the owner approves a `pack_verify` request. Record the correction rate per card (the STORY-45 pass signal is fewer than 1 in 5 facts corrected).

**Missing:** licensing steps per municipality, the Civil Code warranty periods for building defects, autos de medição payment terms under the Public Contracts Code, rental rules and golden-visa changes.
