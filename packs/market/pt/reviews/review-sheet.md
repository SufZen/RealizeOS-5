## How to review (about 60 minutes)

An AI agent drafted this pack from public sources. It is labelled verification level 1 (a draft). Your review lets it be marked as reviewed (level 2), with your name on each card you review.

For each card, check:

1. Is the statement correct and current for Portugal?
2. Does the source support it? A better or official source is welcome.
3. Is anything important missing, misleading or out of date?

Then fill in:

- verdict: correct, corrected or removed.
- facts corrected: how many separate facts you changed (0 if correct). facts checked is our estimate of the facts in the statement; change it if you count differently.
- corrected statement: your wording, if you corrected the card.
- reviewer note: anything else, such as a better source or an exception.

The glossary and defaults rows are optional. Your name, title and registration number are recorded on every card you review.

### 1. pt.contracts.complementary-works

**Statement:** In public works, complementary works (trabalhos complementares) are works of a kind or quantity not in the contract that are needed to carry it out (Article 370 of the Public Contracts Code); they are approved by the owner, usually by an addendum, within legal limits.

> Log every requested complementary work as a Change with its price basis (contract prices where they exist). The Court of Auditors noted that complementary works cannot be offset against works not done. Source is an opinion article; confirm against the consolidated Code.

- Source: <https://eco.sapo.pt/opiniao/os-trabalhos-complementares-nas-empreitadas-de-obras-publicas>

### 2. pt.contractors.alvara

**Statement:** Construction companies need an alvará issued by IMPIC under Law 41/2015 to carry out construction works; its class caps the value of works the company may take.

> Before levelling quotes, check each contractor's alvará and class against the value of the works. Class thresholds are set by ordinance (Portaria 212/2022 per secondary sources) and change; check the current one.

### 6. pt.contracts.price-revision

**Statement:** Price revision (revisão de preços) in Portuguese construction contracts follows Decree-Law 6/2004, in force with amendments (last in 2021); for public works, Article 382 of the Public Contracts Code makes ordinary revision mandatory.

> When a claim includes revision, check the method and formula the contract names (Article 5 of Decree-Law 6/2004) and the base month (the month before the tender deadline in public works). Private contracts apply it only if the contract says so.

- Source: <https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/legislacao-consolidada/decreto-lei/2004-169820399>

### 9. pt.taxes.stamp-duty-on-purchase

### 10. pt.vat.rates

**Statement:** Mainland Portugal's standard VAT (IVA) rate is 23%. A 6% rate (List I, item 2.23 of the VAT Code) applies to building rehabilitation works in urban rehabilitation areas; the Supreme Administrative Court's uniformizing ruling 3/2026 narrowed when it applies.

> Agents state the IVA rate and the reason for it on every amount. Never apply 6% on a project's say-so: flag it for the contabilista, because ruling 3/2026 (Diário da República, 17 March 2026) may require an approved urban rehabilitation operation.
> The source is a Tax Authority binding ruling on item 2.23 after Lei 56/2023.

- Source: <https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/informacoes_vinculativas/despesa/civa/Documents/PIV_29545.pdf>
- As of: 2026-10-13; review by: 2027-04-13
