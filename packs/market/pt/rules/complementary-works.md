---
id: pt.contracts.complementary-works
statement: In public works, complementary works (trabalhos complementares) are works of a kind or quantity
  not in the contract that are needed to carry it out (Article 370 of the Public Contracts Code); they
  are approved by the owner, usually by an addendum, within legal limits.
applies_to:
  family: change
  kinds:
  - variation

as_of: '2026-10-13'
review_by: '2027-04-13'
verification: 1
verified_by: null
---
Log every requested complementary work as a Change with its price basis (contract prices where they exist). The Court of Auditors noted that complementary works cannot be offset against works not done. Source is an opinion article; confirm against the consolidated Code.
