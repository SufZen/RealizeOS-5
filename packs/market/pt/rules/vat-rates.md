statement: Mainland Portugal's standard VAT (IVA) rate is 23%. A 6% rate (List I, item 2.23 of the VAT
  Code) applies to building rehabilitation works in urban rehabilitation areas; the Supreme Administrative
  Court's uniformizing ruling 3/2026 narrowed when it applies.

Agents state the IVA rate and the reason for it on every amount. Never apply 6% on a project's say-so: flag it for the contabilista, because ruling 3/2026 (Diário da República, 17 March 2026) may require an approved urban rehabilitation operation.
The source is a Tax Authority binding ruling on item 2.23 after Lei 56/2023.
