# Spain market pack

Spanish and English terms, defaults (EUR, Monday to Friday, es-ES) and ten rule cards on IVA for housing and renovation, building liability periods, subcontracting (REA, chain limits), energy certificates, price revision and payments on account in public works, licences, and regional transfer taxes.

**Verification: level 1 (drafted by an agent).** Several sources are secondary; each card says where. No Spanish professional has reviewed this pack.

**Missing:** regional (autonomous community) variations, private contract practice, CTE technical requirements, the Libro del Edificio, and payment terms under the late-payment law.

**Who could review it:** an abogado for liability and contracts, a tax adviser for IVA and ITP, and an arquitecto or aparejador for licences and the CTE.
