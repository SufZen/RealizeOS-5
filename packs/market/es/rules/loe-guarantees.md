statement: Under the Building Act (Ley 38/1999 de Ordenación de la Edificación, Article 17), building
  agents answer for material damage for 10 years (structural defects), 3 years (habitability defects)
  and 1 year (finishing defects), counted from acceptance of the works without reservations.

  family: issue

Once damage appears within the period, the claim must be brought within two years (Supreme Court ruling 1211/2023). Record the acceptance date as the start of every period.
