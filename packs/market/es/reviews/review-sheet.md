## How to review (about 60 minutes)

An AI agent drafted this pack from public sources. It is labelled verification level 1 (a draft). Your review lets it be marked as reviewed (level 2), with your name on each card you review.

For each card, check:

1. Is the statement correct and current for Spain?
2. Does the source support it? A better or official source is welcome.
3. Is anything important missing, misleading or out of date?

Then fill in:

- verdict: correct, corrected or removed.
- facts corrected: how many separate facts you changed (0 if correct). facts checked is our estimate of the facts in the statement; change it if you count differently.
- corrected statement: your wording, if you corrected the card.
- reviewer note: anything else, such as a better source or an exception.

The glossary and defaults rows are optional. Your name, title and registration number are recorded on every card you review.

### 3. es.loe.liability-periods

**Statement:** Under the Building Act (Ley 38/1999 de Ordenación de la Edificación, Article 17), building agents answer for material damage for 10 years (structural defects), 3 years (habitability defects) and 1 year (finishing defects), counted from acceptance of the works without reservations.

> Once damage appears within the period, the claim must be brought within two years (Supreme Court ruling 1211/2023). Record the acceptance date as the start of every period.

- Source: <https://www.congreso.es/constitucion/ficheros/leyes_espa/l_038_1999.pdf>

### 5. es.contracts.price-revision

**Statement:** In Spanish public contracts, periodic price revision (Article 103 of Law 9/2017) applies only where the tender documents provide it, covers only raw materials, intermediate goods and energy, and never overheads or profit; Law 11/2023 widened it to all contract types.

> Check the formula and official index named in the pliego before accepting a revised claim.

### 9. es.vat.housing

**Statement:** In Spain, the first delivery of a new dwelling by the developer pays 10% IVA (Article 91.Uno.1.7 of Law 37/1992), protected housing (VPO, special regime) 4%, and commercial premises, offices and most construction for companies the general 21%.

> Up to two parking spaces and annexes sold with the dwelling share the 10% rate. Secondary source; confirm with AEAT binding rulings. The Canary Islands use IGIC instead.

- Source: <https://motordepresupuestos.com/iva-obra-nueva>

### 10. es.vat.renovation-10

**Statement:** Renovation works on a private dwelling can carry 10% IVA instead of 21% only when conditions are met (private residential use, the dwelling more than two years old, materials below the legal share); otherwise 21% applies to the whole job.

> Check the conditions on every renovation quote; secondary source.
