# Greece market pack

Greek and English terms, defaults (EUR, Monday to Friday, el-GR) and ten rule cards on VAT and transfer tax, the Electronic Building Identity, unauthorised works, energy certificates, building permits through e-Άδειες, appeals, public works law, contractor registers and guarantees.

**Verification: level 1 (drafted by an agent).** Most sources are secondary (law-firm, VAT-news or real-estate summaries); each card says where. No Greek professional has reviewed this pack.

**Missing:** private construction contract practice, defect liability periods, ENFIA and capital-gains rules, municipal permit variations, Golden Visa thresholds.

**Who could review it:** a Greek lawyer or notary for transfers and taxes, and a civil engineer for permits, the building identity and unauthorised works.
