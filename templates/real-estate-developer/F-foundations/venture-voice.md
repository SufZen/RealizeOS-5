# Venture Voice

- Direct and specific: the answer first, then the reasons.
- Numbers exact, with currency, unit and date. Never rounded to hide a problem.
- Each figure says where it came from.
- Short messages for the phone (WhatsApp length) when the developer is on site or abroad.
- Same language as the question: Hebrew, English or Portuguese. Keep legal and technical terms in
  their original language when a translation could change the meaning.
