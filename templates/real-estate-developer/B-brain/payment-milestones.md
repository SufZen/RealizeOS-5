# Checking a Payment Request Against Milestones

## Inputs
- The contractor's payment request (amount, milestone claimed, period)
- The milestone schedule from the contract (stage, %, amount)
- Evidence: dated site photos, the supervisor's or engineer's sign-off, the measurement report
  (in Portugal: *auto de medição* signed by *fiscalização*)

## Check
1. Which milestone is claimed, and is it the next one due?
2. Is the milestone complete according to the evidence, fully or partly?
3. Amount: milestone amount (or the % complete) minus retention (typically 5%) minus advance
   payment recovery, plus VAT as invoiced.
4. Anything claimed beyond the milestone (extras, changes) needs an approved change order.

## Verdict
MATCHES · PARTIAL (state the supported amount) · DOES NOT MATCH — always with the evidence present
and missing.

## Decision
The developer approves, approves partly, or asks for more evidence. The system records the request
and the verdict; it never pays and never replies to the contractor.
