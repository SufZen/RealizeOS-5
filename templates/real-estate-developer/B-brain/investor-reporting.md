# Quarterly Investor Report

## Sections
1. **Summary** — three sentences: where we are, money, the one thing to watch
2. **Progress vs plan** — milestones reached, delays and why
3. **Budget** — budget, committed, paid, remaining, contingency used, net remaining
4. **Risks** — each with its likely impact and what we are doing about it
5. **Next quarter** — milestones, payments due, decisions needed

## Figures
Every figure states its source: the sheet and range, or the knowledge-base document. Forecasts are
labelled with their assumption. Totals must add up.

## Tone
Plain and specific. Bad news early and with a plan. No promises the developer hasn't made.

## Sending
The report is a draft for the developer. Nothing goes to investors without them.
