# Development Project Lifecycle

## 1. Opportunity and feasibility
Land or building identified; zoning and permitted area checked; feasibility with worst case and
sensitivity (sale price, construction cost, timeline). Decision: acquire or pass.

## 2. Planning and permits
Architect and consultants appointed; design stages; permit application. In Portugal the municipal
licensing process (*licenciamento*) sets the critical path.

## 3. Tender and contractor selection
Tender documents issued (specifications, bill of quantities, contract conditions, guarantees,
insurance). Bids received, levelled against the BoQ, clarified, negotiated. Contract signed.

## 4. Execution
Works by milestones. Each payment request is checked against the milestone schedule, the
supervisor's sign-off and dated site evidence. Retention is held until completion; changes are
priced and approved before they are built.

## 5. Sales and handover
Pricing, reservations, promissory contracts, deeds. Snag lists and handover.

## 6. Capital and investors
Equity and debt per the capital plan. Investors receive a quarterly report: progress vs plan,
budget vs actual, cost to complete, risks, next quarter.

## Decision rights
The developer decides: contractor selection, every payment, changes, anything sent to investors.
The team prepares, checks and records.
