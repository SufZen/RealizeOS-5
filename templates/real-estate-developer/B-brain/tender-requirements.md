# Reading a Tender — Requirements Register

## What to extract
- **Administrative:** registration, certificates, declarations, signatures, format and language
- **Technical:** specifications, standards, methods, team and experience
- **Financial:** price format, BoQ completion, payment terms, financial statements
- **Guarantees and insurance:** bid bond, performance guarantee, amounts and validity
- **Legal:** contract conditions, penalties, dispute resolution
- **Dates:** clarification questions, site visit, submission, opening, guarantee validity

## Register columns
section · requirement (original wording) · mandatory (yes / no / check) · owner · due date · source

## Rules
- A requirement is mandatory only when the text says so ("must", "shall", "under penalty of
  exclusion"). Otherwise mark "check".
- Every row names its document and section.
- Contradictions between documents are listed separately for a clarification question.
- The register is a working tool; the decision to bid belongs to the developer.
